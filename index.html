Good to Know
What should I wear?
Something suitable for a church ceremony and an outdoor backyard reception. There is no strict dress code. Comfortable shoes are encouraged for the backyard setting.
Where are the ceremony and reception?
Ceremony: Mary Immaculate Roman Catholic Church
16 Browns Ln, Bellport, NY 11713

Reception: Bellport Inn
160 S Country Rd, Bellport, NY 11713
Where should I park?
Street parking is available near Bellport Inn. There is also a parking lot between Bellport Ln and Bell St, about one block from both the church and the reception.
What can I do during free time?
From 1:00–3:00 PM, feel free to join us for a walk to the marina, explore nearby, grab a drink, or simply relax. If you return to Bellport Inn early, drinks and live trio music will begin around 2:00 PM.
Where are the bathrooms?
There are two bathrooms on the first floor, just inside the entrance under the wisteria walkway, one on each side of the kitchen. There is also one bathroom upstairs in the main building, at the end of the hallway.
Is the reception outdoors?
Yes. The reception will take place outdoors at Bellport Inn in the backyard and tented area.
